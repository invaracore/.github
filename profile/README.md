### The problem

Shorting a stock carries a hidden cost: the **borrow fee**. When a stock becomes hard to borrow, that fee can climb past 50%, even past 1,000% a year, and most traders only see it when they place the trade.

### What we build

| | |
|---|---|
| 🔍 **Borrow Screener** | Live borrow fees and share availability across 18,000+ US stocks and ETFs. |
| 📈 **Stock pages** | Fee, rank, cost-to-short calculator and live price for every ticker. |
| 🖥️ **Quant Terminal** | Borrow fees, prices and overnight gap risk in one real-time view. |
| 🛰️ **Oracle Status** | Live health of every on-chain equity price feed on Robinhood Chain. |
| 🏦 **INVARA Vault** *(coming soon)* | Lock stablecoins, mint synthetic equities. Non-custodial, no admin keys, 0% vault fees. |

### Principles

- **Non-custodial.** Your collateral stays in an immutable contract. Nobody, including us, can move it.
- **No admin keys.** No pause switch, no upgrade proxy, no hidden controls.
- **Real data only.** Every number on the site comes from a live source.
